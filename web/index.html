Clinical scoring reference • Bandim TBscore II

TBscore II

Eight-point clinical severity score for monitoring people undergoing treatment for pulmonary tuberculosis.

Research / educational use. The score does not diagnose TB, establish drug resistance, determine emergency triage, or replace clinical assessment.

Assessment

Enter all six findings from the same assessment. No patient identifiers are needed.

Symptoms and clinical sign
Anthropometry

BMI: 1 point if below 18, plus 1 if below 16. MUAC: 1 point if below 220 mm, plus 1 if below 200 mm. Cutoffs are strictly below.

Optional treatment comparison

Compares baseline with the current assessment. The research evaluated whether the score declined by at least 25% at approximately two months of treatment; it does not provide an individualized risk estimate.

Scoring and interpretation

Each present symptom or sign contributes one point. BMI and MUAC each contribute up to two points; total score ranges from 0 to 8.

Evidence and limitations

The scoring items and severity classes follow Rudolf et al. (2013), evaluated in cohorts from Guinea-Bissau and Ethiopia. Prognostic performance varied between cohorts. This is not a substitute for microbiological testing, clinical review or treatment monitoring protocols.

Rudolf et al., Scand J Infect Dis (2013) — DOI 10.3109/00365548.2013.826876
